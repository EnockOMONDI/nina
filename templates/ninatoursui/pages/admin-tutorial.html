01. Admin Basics

Start here each morning to get organized and see what needs attention first.

Steps

  1. Open the Admin Dashboard.
  2. Review new inquiries and applications.
  3. List what you will handle first.

Do / Don’t

Do: begin with the newest requests. Don’t: publish changes before a quick review.

02. Packages and Pricing

Use this section whenever package details, prices, tables, or photos need to be updated.

Steps

  1. Open the package record.
  2. Update text and pricing details.
  3. Save and confirm the page looks right on the live site.

Do / Don’t

Do: keep headings clear and easy to read. Don’t: leave repeated hotel options or itinerary lines.

Scenario: A customer asks for a Mara quote. Open the Mara package, confirm the current rates, then share the updated quote.

03. Hotels and Images

Use this section when hotel photos, prices, or details need a refresh.

Steps

  1. Find the hotel record.
  2. Update the image and price.
  3. Check the hotel card on the website.

Do / Don’t

Do: use clear hotel names. Don’t: keep duplicate hotel records active.

Scenario: A hotel image looks wrong. Replace it, save, refresh the page, and confirm the correct photo appears.

Action Tip: When marketing shares a new approved image, update it the same day.

04. Inquiries and Follow-up

This section helps you respond to customer inquiries and close each case clearly.

Steps

  1. Open the new inquiry.
  2. Reply with clear package or hotel details.
  3. Mark it as resolved after follow-up.

Do / Don’t

Do: reply the same day where possible. Don’t: leave inquiries without an update.

Scenario: For a hotel inquiry, first confirm travel dates, room preference, and budget, then send the quote.

05. Careers and Content Updates

Use this section to review CVs, manage job applications, and keep content up to date.

Steps

  1. Open new applications.
  2. Review details and documents.
  3. Share with the right team and update status.

Do / Don’t

Do: keep records neat and easy to follow. Don’t: publish content before reviewing it.

Scenario: A CV comes in from the careers page. Review it, forward it to the jobs team, and update the status.
